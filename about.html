Rubex UI was founded with one conviction: the best web work happens when engineering rigor meets thoughtful design. We're a small team with a large footprint.

Who We Are

A studio built on craft and clarity

Rubex UI is a boutique web development agency headquartered in Kyiv, Ukraine. We partner with startups, scale-ups, and established brands to design and build digital products that perform — technically and commercially.

Since 2019, we've shipped over 50 projects across 20+ countries. Our clients span fintech, SaaS, e-commerce, and media — united by a shared insistence on quality.

We're not an agency in the traditional sense. We operate more like an embedded product team: opinionated, outcome-focused, and invested in your success beyond the delivery date.
