Common Questions

Before you write

How long does a project take?

Most marketing sites take 4–8 weeks. Web applications vary — typically 2–5 months depending on scope.

Do you work with startups?

Yes. Some of our best work has been for early-stage companies who needed to move fast and build right the first time.

What if I only have an idea?

That's a fine place to start. We run paid discovery workshops to help you define scope, tech stack, and budget before committing.

How do payments work?

We typically work on a 50% upfront / 50% on delivery model, or milestone-based for larger projects.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes. Monthly maintenance plans are available from $500/month and include updates, monitoring, and a support allowance.

What timezone do you work in?

We're based in Kyiv (EET / UTC+2). We overlap comfortably with EU hours and can accommodate US East Coast mornings.
